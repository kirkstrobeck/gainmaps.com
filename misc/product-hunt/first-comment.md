## Recommended

Hey Product Hunt — Kirk here, maker of Gainmaps.

SDR JPEGs clip highlights to reference white. Phones and HDR displays can show more, but most files never carry that extra range.

Gainmaps encodes a gain-map JPEG in your browser. Drop a JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, HEIC, or SVG. You get one Ultra HDR JPEG: an SDR base every app can open, plus a gain map for HDR displays. Nothing uploads. No account. No server.

Try it in about twenty seconds: open https://gainmaps.com and drop a photo. Or browse /logos and /photos and toggle Standard vs Ultra on an HDR display.

Honest caveat: Ultra highlights only appear on HDR-capable displays (Apple XDR, Android Ultra HDR, Windows Advanced Color). On SDR the file looks like a normal JPEG. That is expected.

If you want a CLI: brew install gainmap

Free and open source: https://github.com/kirkstrobeck/gainmaps.com

I would love feedback on encoding quality, browsers that fail, and files that break — more useful than anything else.
